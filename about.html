ABOUT THE COLLECTION, 2026

A COMMON THREAD

A collection of things I've made, and the materials that connect them.

MADE BY HAND. CONNECTED BY MATERIAL.

A Common Thread is a digital archive of objects I've designed and made, from everyday accessories to experimental forms and typographic explorations.

While each project began with a different idea and to serve a different purpose, many share the same materials, techniques, or creative processes. This collection brings those relationships together, exploring how a single material can take on entirely different forms.

FOLLOW THE CONNECTIONS

Rather than organizing the work chronologically or by project type, this archive invites you to explore through connections.

Each circle represents an object or material. Solid lines reveal shared physical materials, while dotted lines connect related projects and creative explorations. Follow any thread to discover how the pieces relate.

BEHIND THE PROJECT

I'm Julia Salinero, a designer studying Computer Science and Graphic Design at Lehigh University. My work moves between digital design and physical making, often exploring how materials, functionality, and experimentation influence the final outcome.

This archive is a way of looking beyond the finished objects and recognizing the processes and connections that brought them together.
