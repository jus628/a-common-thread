ETERNA PACKAGING

2025

BEYOND THE BRACELET

Designed to accompany the Eterna Bracelet, this handmade chipboard box extends the wearable design into its presentation. Featuring an engraved L&J logo, a magnetic closure, and a cushioned interior, the packaging was created to protect and display the bracelet while reflecting the brand's balance of functionality and elegance.

Like the bracelet itself, the box transforms simple materials into something both practical and thoughtfully designed.
