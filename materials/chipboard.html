CHIPBOARD

Chipboard is a lightweight, versatile material made from compressed paper fibers. Easy to cut, shape, and assemble, it can be transformed into anything from small accessories to larger structural forms. Across this collection, chipboard becomes the foundation for objects that experiment with both function and form.
